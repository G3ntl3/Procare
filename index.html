A-101 · StudioDrawn by: the people who build itRev. C · 2026

One team designs it, prices it and builds it, so the drawings and the building match on handover day.

Axen Form started in 1998 as six carpenters and an architect sharing a yard in Deptford. We now run 180 people across design, estimating and site, and we still don't subcontract the work we're known for: frames, carpentry and the final fix.

Clients get one contract, one programme and one person who answers the phone from the first sketch to the last snag.

A-201 · ServicesSix disciplines, one contractScale 1:50

From bare ground to keys in hand.

Pick one service or hand us the whole job. Either way, the same site manager stays with you until handover.

  • One contract from first sketch to handover. Our architects and site managers plan the building at the same table.

  • Timber-frame and masonry homes, from single plots to 120-unit schemes, built to Future Homes Standard.

  • Concrete and steel frames for offices, retail podiums and residential towers up to twelve storeys.

  • Strip-outs, structural repair and deep energy retrofit for buildings worth keeping.

  • First fix to final snag: services, joinery, finishes and the commissioning paperwork.

  • Site clearance, piling, drainage and foundations, planned around your neighbours.

S-301 · Build sequenceScroll to build · drag to orbitNot to scale

Watch a building go up.

  1. 01Groundwork

    Hoarding, crane base, piles and pad footings. We set out the grid to ±3 mm.

  2. 02Frame & floors

    Columns, beams and precast planks, one level every nine working days.

  3. 03Envelope

    Curtain wall and fins go on as soon as each floor is signed off, so trades can work in the dry.

  4. 04Roof & plant

    Parapets, plant enclosure and 84 PV panels. The crane comes down.

  5. 05Handover

    Lights on, landscaping in, 46 weekly photo diaries in your inbox.

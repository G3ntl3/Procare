S-301 · Build sequenceScroll to build · drag to orbitProcare Builders

Watch a building take shape.

  1. 01Groundwork & setting out

    Site hoarding, foundations, pad footings and structural grid laid out to exact tolerances.

  2. 02Frame & structural works

    Reinforced concrete columns, beams, floor slabs and core erected level by level.

  3. 03Cladding & envelope

    Curtain walling, architectural cladding panels and exterior fins lock in the facade.

  4. 04Roof, metalwork & plant

    Parapets, structural steel louvres, rooftop services and solar array completed.

  5. 05Illumination & handover

    Hoarding struck, exterior lighting powered on, and finished building handed over.
